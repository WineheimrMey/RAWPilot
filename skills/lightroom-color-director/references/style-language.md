# Style Language

Define a look with observable attributes, not a preset label alone. Use five axes:

1. **Tonal key:** high-key, balanced, or low-key; black floor; highlight rolloff; contrast shape.
2. **Palette:** neutral, warm, cool, complementary, or analogous; dominant and accent hues.
3. **Saturation distribution:** global restraint versus selective color emphasis.
4. **Texture:** clean, crisp, soft, tactile, grainy, or polished.
5. **Spatial hierarchy:** which subject/region leads and which regions recede.

Keep technical correction invariant when changing style strength. Scale curve, grading, selected HSL, grain, and vignette; do not scale lens correction, clipping recovery, skin protection, or noise control.

## Style Strength

- **Subtle:** retain documentary color; use a mild curve, narrow palette shift, and barely visible texture.
- **Balanced:** make the palette and hierarchy clearly intentional without sacrificing believable color.
- **Strong:** allow an obvious art-directed look, but retain subject readability, plausible skin/materials, controlled clipping, and clean mask edges.

## Clean Editorial / Natural Commercial

- Signature: neutral-to-gently warm balance, open midtones, clean whites, restrained saturation, crisp but natural detail.
- Best for: brand, lifestyle, product-adjacent portraits, food, travel editorial.
- Prioritize: material/skin fidelity, uncluttered hierarchy, consistent exposure.
- Reject: gray whites, plastic skin, fluorescent colors, obvious vignette, stylized cross-processing.

## Bright and Airy

- Signature: luminous midtones/highlights, soft contrast, clean pastel color, light shadow density.
- Best for: wedding daylight, family, newborn, lifestyle, hospitality.
- Prioritize: highlight rolloff, white clothing detail, gentle skin luminosity.
- Reject: clipped dresses/windows, gray blacks everywhere, washed-out faces, cyan shadows.

## Moody Low-Key

- Signature: restrained average brightness, protected highlight accents, deep but separated shadows, limited palette.
- Best for: environmental portrait, night, fashion, narrative architecture.
- Prioritize: readable key subject and a deliberate black floor.
- Reject: dead-black subject regions, global underexposure, muddy skin, heavy vignette stacking.

## Restrained Cinematic

- Signature: shaped light hierarchy, smooth highlight rolloff, modest complementary separation, selective saturation, cohesive texture.
- Best for: narrative portrait, street, night, travel, editorial.
- Prioritize: motivated warm/cool relationship and scene-specific dominant colors.
- Reject: automatic teal-orange, crushed blacks, cyan skin, every lamp made orange, excessive dehaze.

## Warm Gold / Soft Ember / Rich Gold

- Signature: warm highlight family, controlled red/orange, soft-to-rich contrast depending on strength, nostalgic but credible neutrals.
- Best for: golden hour, wedding, food, autumn, warm interiors.
- Prioritize: skin and white-object neutrality while warming luminous regions.
- Reject: yellow whites, orange skin, brown foliage, warmth applied uniformly to shadows.

## Faded Blue / Deep Blue / Cool Editorial

- Signature: cool dominant palette with either lifted/faded blacks or deeper clean shadows; warm accents remain selective.
- Best for: urban, architecture, rain, night, fashion.
- Prioritize: neutral skin/materials and separation between blue, aqua, and green.
- Reject: cyan skin, blue neutrals, crushed subject shadows, fluorescent teal.

## Muted Earth / Sage

- Signature: restrained greens/yellows, warm neutrals, tactile midtones, quiet saturation.
- Best for: outdoor portrait, lifestyle, travel, botanical, rustic food.
- Prioritize: foliage hue discipline and natural skin separation.
- Reject: gray dead foliage, olive skin, muddy midtones, a universal green cast.

## Documentary True-to-Life

- Signature: plausible available light, moderate contrast, faithful local color, minimal visible grading.
- Best for: reportage, events, travel, family archives.
- Prioritize: moment, faces, temporal continuity, and honest mixed-light cues.
- Reject: stylization that changes the perceived time/place, over-neutralized practical lights, artificial subject spotlighting.

## Luxury Fashion / Polished Editorial

- Signature: controlled blacks, precise neutrals, selective rich color, polished texture, graphic separation.
- Best for: fashion, beauty, jewelry, premium brand imagery.
- Prioritize: wardrobe/product color, skin luminosity, specular form, clean background.
- Reject: cheap-looking oversaturation, crunchy microcontrast, lost fabric detail, mismatched skin across a set.

## Vintage / Matte Film-Inspired

- Signature: softened contrast, intentional color bias, restrained saturation, coherent grain, gently compressed endpoints.
- Best for: lifestyle, travel, editorial, memory-driven narrative.
- Prioritize: one coherent palette and texture scale.
- Reject: haze over the whole image, milky blacks that erase depth, digital noise plus heavy grain, random hue shifts.

## Black and White

- Choose **clean tonal**, **silver-rich**, **warm/cool muted**, or **high-contrast noir** explicitly.
- Use B&W Mix to control how original colors map to luminance; do not rely on desaturation alone.
- Protect face/subject separation, highlight texture, and black detail before adding grain.
- Reject blocked eyes/hair, glowing skin masks, halos, noisy flat skies, or identical luminance for formerly distinct colors.

## Translate Ambiguous Requests

- “电影感”: ask or infer tonal key, dominant palette, accent color, subject hierarchy, and strength; never default to teal-orange.
- “高级”: translate into controlled endpoints, restrained saturation, material/skin fidelity, selective contrast, and clean edges.
- “胶片感”: choose warm, cool, faded, rich, or monochrome family; specify grain scale and endpoint behavior.
- “通透”: open useful midtones, clean color contamination, preserve black point and highlight rolloff; do not simply raise Exposure and Clarity.
- “氛围感”: preserve motivated light sources and palette relationships; do not hide the subject or add a global cast.
